Why stability matters

TC1031 · What happens to equal elements when you sort

A sorting algorithm is stable when two elements that compare as equal keep their original relative order after sorting. It sounds like a technicality until you see the two cases below side by side.

The 4-star and 3-star groups came out of alphabetical order. The books are still grouped correctly by rating — sorting still "worked" — but the alphabetical order you already had got shuffled as a side effect, for free, without any warning.

The point isn't the tied values themselves — it's everything else those records carry that you are not currently sorting by. Stability is what lets you sort by one field, then by another, and trust that the first ordering survives inside every group of the second. Lose stability and you lose that guarantee silently: the output still looks "sorted," the bug just shows up later, when someone assumes the titles inside a rating group are still alphabetical and they are not.
